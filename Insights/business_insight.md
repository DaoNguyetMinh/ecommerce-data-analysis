\### BUSINESS INSIGHT



\## 1. Marketplace Growth

&#x09;Marketplace revenue grew rapidly between 2016 and 2018. Revenue increased from 51,813 in 2016 to over 8.5 million in 2018, representing a dramatic expansion of the platform.

&#x09;The largest growth occurred between 2016 and 2017, when revenue increased by more than 13,000% indicating the early scaling phase of the marketplace.

\## 2. Revenue Structure

&#x09;Revenue is highly distributed across the product catalog. The top-performing product contributes only 0.43% of total revenue, while the top 10 products together account for just 3.21%.

This indicates that the marketplace follows a long-tail sales pattern, where a large number of products collectively generate revenue rather than relying on a few dominant best sellers.

\-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Revenue by category: Revenue is relatively diversified across product categories. The top 5 categories contribute approximately 39% of total revenue, with no single category exceeding 10%. this indicates that the business dose not rely heavily on a specific category and benefits from a balanced product mix. Such diversification may reduce risk but also suggests that growth opportunities could come from optimizing multiple categories rather than focusing on a single dominant segment.
* Revenue by customer segment: Revenue is heavily concentrated in one-time customers, who contribute over 94% of total revenue while also representing the vast majority of the customer base. In contrast, repeat and high-value customers account for less than 6% of revenue, indicating extremely low customer retention. This suggests that the business relies almost entirely on new customer acquisition rather than repeat purchases, highlighting a critical opportunity to improve retention and customer lifetime value.
* AOV by month: AOV shows a slight downward trend over time, while both revenue and order volume increase significantly. This indicates that business growth is primarily driven by higher transaction volume rather than increased spending per order. The decline in AOV suggests that customers may be placing smaller orders, potentially due to a shift toward lower-priced products or an increase in new customers with lower spending behavior.
* AOV by category: High-AOV categories such as "pcs" generate relatively low revenue, indicating niche demand with high-value products. In contrast, categories like "beleza\_saude" and "relogios\_presentes" contribute significantly to total revenue despite having moderate AOV, suggesting that business growth is primarily driven by high order volume rather than high-value transactions. This highlights a clear distinction between high-value niche categories and high-volume core categories.
* AOV by customer segment: Revenue is heavily concentrated in one-time customers, who account for the vast majority of both orders and total revenue. Interestingly, one-time customers also exhibit the highest AOV, while repeat and high-value customers show lower spending per order. This suggests that the business lacks strong customer retention, and returning customers do not demonstrate higher purchasing value as typically expected.



* Insight data story from AOV + revenue: Revenue growth is primarily driven by a significant increase in order volume rather than higher spending per order, as AOV shows a slight declining trend over time. This growth is largely fueled by one-time customers, who dominate both total orders and revenue contribution. However, these customers exhibit relatively moderate spending behavior, contributing to the overall decline in AOV. In contrast, repeat and high-value customers represent only a small portion of the customer base and do not demonstrate higher AOV as typically expected. This indicates that the business lacks strong customer retention and fails to generate higher-value transactions from returning customers. Overall, the business appears to rely heavily on acquiring new customers rather than building long-term customer value.

\## 3. Seller Contribution

&#x09;Analysis of the top 5 sellers by revenue shows that 4 of them are located in the state of São Paulo (SP). this suggests that high-performing sellers are geographically concentrated in this region.

&#x09;The dominance of São Paulo sellers may reflect stronger logistics networks, better access to suppliers, or higher marketplace adoption in this area.
